Design a Buffalo House — Part 2: Model It

Interactive Protractor & Sun-Angle Walkthrough (1 ft → 3 ft → 2 ft)
Goal: Block all summer sun in June (71°) so the window gets none, while letting all winter sun in December (24°) hit the whole window (3 ft to 7 ft).
Scale: 2 small squares = 1 ft
1. Draw the Roof Overhang

Start at the black dot at the top of the wall (8 ft). Use a ruler to draw a straight horizontal line left toward South.

2. Place Protractor Upside-Down

Flip the protractor so the curved edge faces DOWN and the flat edge is on TOP. Put the center hole on the outer left tip of the overhang (NOT the wall) with 0° along the overhang to the right.

3. Mark June Sun (71°)

Starting from the 0° right flat edge along the overhang, count 71° downward and make a small dot on the grid.

4. Mark December Sun (24°)

Without moving the protractor, count 24° downward from the same 0° right flat edge and make a second dot.

5. Connect Dots & Check Window

Use a ruler to draw straight rays from the overhang tip through each dot to the wall or ground. Below the line = IN SUN; above = IN SHADE.
